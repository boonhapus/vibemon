---

Vibemon is a cozy monster-battler where the first spark comes from the Trainer's own world.

The sky outside. The neighborhood underfoot. The songs still humming in the room.

Those signals gather into a new Vibemon. You welcome them to your Crew, wander into wild encounters, and battle in a warm pixel world that feels like a beloved cartridge from a gentler 1970s.

Every Hatch is a small keepsake from the moment it arrived.
