# Ideas: Generative Aesthetics & Showcase

## Period: Visual Identity & Pride

### Problem Statement
Vibemon should feel "alive." Static sprite sheets are a great start, but the project needs interactive visuals and a way to memorialize a trainer's journey through their team's history.

---

## 1. AI-Animated Sprites
Move beyond static PNGs to dynamic idle and battle animations.
- **Idle Animations**: Use AI video generation (or procedural CSS transforms) to create breathing, swaying, or floating movements for the "Center" pose.
- **Battle Reactions**: Specific animations for taking damage, attacking, or fainting.
- **Elemental Auras**: Purchasable overlays (Fire, Sparkles, Dark) that respond to the Vibemon's type and role.

---

## 2. Alumni Roster (Showcase)
A dedicated UI to view all Vibemon ever owned, including those released or traded.
- **Trophies & Titles**: Display milestones (e.g., "First Mythical," "Champion of Route 7").
- **Stat Tracking**: Total wins, damage dealt, and XP earned across the Vibemon's entire history.
- **Export for Socials**: A "Share" button that generates a polished "Trainer Card" with the current team's aesthetics and stats.

---

## 3. Aesthetic Customization
Non-pay-to-win items available in the shop for Vibe Gold.
- **Shiny Spray**: A permanent palette-swap overlay.
- **Battle Cry Remix**: Re-prompting the generative audio engine for a new style.
- **Showcase Frames**: Rare borders for the Alumni Roster or Trainer Card.
