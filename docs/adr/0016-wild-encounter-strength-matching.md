# Encounter matching uses party strength with variance

Encounter matching considers **Party Strength** after geography has established the eligible wild pool. The same strength estimate is used for trainer-vs-trainer matching, so it describes a full party rather than only a single wild matchup. The initial member strength formula is actual stat total: HP, attack, defense, special attack, special defense, and speed after level scaling. Initial party strength is average member strength plus 25% of the highest member strength plus 10% of total member strength, so it accounts for both individual power and roster depth. PvP compares party strength directly between trainers. For wild 6v1 matching, the wild Vibemon's member strength is compared against an initial target of 45% of the trainer's party strength, accepting candidates from 70% to 140% of that target and weighting toward the target; this 45% reduction is wild-only. These numbers are encounter tuning constants and should start as centralized code constants so balance work can change them easily in reviewed code; config or admin-backed tuning is deferred until live balance operations need it. Released high-level Vibemon remain in the wild pool, but their eligibility or encounter weight should account for the encountering trainer's party progression while still allowing variance, including encounters the trainer may lose.
