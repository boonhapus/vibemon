# Wild pool is scoped by birth geography

Wild Vibemon are selected from a pool anchored by their birth latitude and longitude, using coarse geospatial buckets as the primary encounter eligibility mechanism. The initial bucket uses geohash precision 5; sparse areas expand to neighboring buckets before new candidates are generated, and distance may influence encounter weighting inside the eligible bucket set. New generation for encounter supply creates christened **Wild** Vibemon directly rather than entering trainer candidate review, with manifestation deferred to prewarm or encounter preparation. Non-geographic provider context shapes the Vibemon but does not gate future encounter eligibility. This keeps weather and other provider-born creatures geographically coherent without making every provider dimension part of encounter matching while still filling thin local supply over time.
