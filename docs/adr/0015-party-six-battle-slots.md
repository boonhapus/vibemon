# Trainer party has six battle slots

A trainer's battle party has six slots, and at this stage those six slots are the trainer's full owned roster. Adoption is free once a candidate is shown, but if adoption would put a seventh Vibemon into the trainer's party, the trainer must release one of the existing six party Vibemon. That full-party adoption is an atomic swap: release the selected party Vibemon, adopt the new Vibemon, and assign the freed battle slot in one transaction. Storage/box ownership is deferred to a future stage so release and party-cap rules stay simple now.
